The Problem

Finding local help shouldn't be this complicated.

  • Students want flexible ways to earn money with skills they already have, without committing to a rigid part-time job.
  • Clients have no trusted place to find local help. They rely on social media and word of mouth without reviews.
  • International freelancing platforms don't fit Algeria, especially when it comes to local payment methods.

The Solution: Tasky

A dedicated local platform connecting skills and needs directly. No unnecessary middlemen, no hassle. Just the right people at the right time.

How It Works

Four simple steps.

From posting a task to the final mutual review.

1

Post

The client posts the task: description, budget, deadline, and location.

2

Apply

Providers browse and apply, or the client directly selects a provider.

3

Deliver

Both sides agree, discuss in chat, and complete the task smoothly.

4

Review

Both sides leave a rating and review. Community trust grows task by task.

Good to know

Tasky connects people directly. Payments are agreed upon and handled directly between users, not processed in-app. Verification badges (student ID, national ID) indicate identity checks were performed; they do not constitute an absolute guarantee of work quality.
